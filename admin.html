Duplicate-account signal (same signup IP)
⚠️ This is a SIGNAL, not proof. It groups accounts that shared a public IP address at the moment they signed up. A VPN or app restart easily changes a user's IP, so this misses determined abusers — and it can also flag genuine users on the same office/home WiFi or the same mobile carrier NAT as false positives. Use it to decide who to look at more closely, not as automatic grounds for action.
